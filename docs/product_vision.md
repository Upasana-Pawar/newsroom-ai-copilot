# Product Vision

## Project Name

Newsroom AI Copilot

## Vision Statement

Newsroom AI Copilot empowers journalists and editorial teams by automating research, verification, content ideation, and social media adaptation while maintaining editorial trust, transparency, and human oversight.

## Mission Statement

Transform a topic, article, or document into verified, publication-ready insights and multi-channel content within minutes.

---

## Product Goals

The primary goals of this project are:

- Reduce time spent on repetitive editorial tasks
- Improve access to trustworthy information
- Support journalists with AI-assisted workflows
- Generate content suitable for multiple publishing channels
- Demonstrate modern AI engineering practices using multi-agent systems

---

## Target Users

- Journalists
- News Editors
- Content Teams
- Social Media Producers
- Digital Publishers

---

## Core Value Proposition

Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots, Newsroom AI Copilot provides a structured editorial workflow powered by multiple specialized AI agents.

The platform helps users:

- Research topics
- Verify information
- Generate headlines
- Review content quality
- Create platform-specific social media content

through a single integrated workflow.

---

## Success Criteria

A successful MVP should:

- Generate research briefs from topics, URLs, or uploaded documents
- Produce source-backed summaries
- Create multiple headline styles
- Generate social media content automatically
- Provide fact-checking assistance
- Demonstrate multi-agent orchestration using LangGraph
