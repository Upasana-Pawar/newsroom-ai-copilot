# Business Problem

## Background

Modern newsrooms operate under significant time pressure.

Journalists are expected to:

- Research complex topics
- Verify information
- Build timelines
- Draft content
- Create headlines
- Adapt content for multiple platforms

These activities consume valuable time that could otherwise be spent on investigative reporting and editorial decision-making.

---

## Problem Statement

While AI tools such as ChatGPT can assist with content creation, journalists still need to manually orchestrate research, verification, headline generation, and content adaptation.

This results in:

- Fragmented workflows
- Repetitive tasks
- Increased cognitive load
- Reduced efficiency

---

## Why Existing Solutions Are Limited

### Traditional Workflow

Research → Verification → Writing → Editing → Social Distribution

Each step is performed separately.

### General-Purpose Chatbots

General-purpose AI tools require users to:

- Manage prompts manually
- Coordinate multiple tasks
- Verify outputs independently
- Reformat content for different channels

---

## Proposed Solution

Newsroom AI Copilot provides a multi-agent workflow that automates key editorial tasks while maintaining human oversight.

The system combines:

- Research
- Fact-checking
- Headline generation
- Editorial review
- Social content creation

into a unified workflow.

---

## Expected Business Benefits

- Faster content production
- Reduced repetitive work
- Improved editorial consistency
- Better source transparency
- Increased productivity
